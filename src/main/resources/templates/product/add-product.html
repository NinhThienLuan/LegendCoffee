Product Management

Create New Product

Fill all required fields, upload a product image, then submit to create a new product record. This form is connected to ProductRequestDTO and supports multipart image upload.

Quick Rules

  • - Name and origin are required.
  • - Expiry date must be after manufacturer date.
  • - Max image size depends on backend config.

Submit Target

POST /products/add

Accepted: image files. Recommended size: under 5MB.

Expiry date must be after manufacturer date.
